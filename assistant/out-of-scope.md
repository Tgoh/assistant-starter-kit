# Out of scope

Subjects the principal has ruled out of the system. **The rule is: don't record
it, don't raise it, don't ask about it, don't search for it.** Not a filing
decision: it doesn't go in the journal either.

These are the only decisions loaded every session, because an automated sweep
rediscovers an excluded subject on every run unless it checks.

**Each entry names the subject and nothing else**, with the date it was ruled
out. Nothing about why.
